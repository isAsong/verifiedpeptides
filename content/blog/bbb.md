# Understanding Tirzepatide

Tirzepatide is a **dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist** developed for the treatment of type 2 diabetes and obesity. Its unique mechanism of action involves:

- **GIP receptor activation**: Enhances insulin secretion in a glucose-dependent manner.
- **GLP-1 receptor activation**: Suppresses glucagon secretion and slows gastric emptying.

## Clinical Implications

Recent studies have shown that Tirzepatide achieves superior glycemic control compared to selective GLP-1 receptor agonists. This is attributed to the synergistic effect of GIP and GLP-1 co-agonism.
